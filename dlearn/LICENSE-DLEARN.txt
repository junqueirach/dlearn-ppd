Provenance of the copy in dlearn/zace-1997: the "10.06.97" update distributed
on the Internet by ZACE Services Ltd (Lausanne, Switzerland), compiled with
Microsoft Fortran PowerStation 4.0, which adds the 8-node brick element
(BR1.FOR, BR2.FOR) and the Lanczos eigensolver LANSO.FOR (B. Nour-Omid, 1986).
The same distribution is the ancestor of the other DLEARN copies on GitHub.
